## Overview

A sample persona for this demo. They know the product well and come back to it as part of their regular work.

## Goals

- Finish routine tasks quickly.
- Pick up where they left off.
- Solve problems without waiting for someone else.

## Needs

- Shortcuts for actions they repeat often.
- A clear history of what changed.

## Frustrations

- Repeating the same steps every time.
- Losing work when something goes wrong.

## Typical scenario

1. Opens the product and resumes the last task.
2. Completes it with a shortcut.
3. Checks the result and moves on.
