## Overview

A sample persona for this demo. They found the product through a recommendation and have a few minutes to decide whether it is worth their time.

## Goals

- Understand what the product does in a minute.
- Reach a first useful result in a single session.
- Know what to do next.

## Needs

- Clear labels and a short path to the first result.
- Examples they can start from instead of an empty page.

## Frustrations

- Long forms before seeing any value.
- Terms they have never heard before.

## Typical scenario

1. Opens the product from a link.
2. Creates an account.
3. Starts from an example and completes a first task.
